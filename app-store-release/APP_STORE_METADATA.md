```text
How are you feeling right now? Not just "fine" or "okay," but truly?

"How Am I?" is a gentle, science-based mood and emotion tracker designed to help you build emotional literacy and self-awareness — without ads, without accounts, and without your data ever leaving your iPhone.

Just 30 seconds a day to pause, take a deep breath, and accurately name what is happening inside.

◆ ROOTED IN THE YALE RULER METHOD & 4 QUADRANTS
The app is based on the circumplex model of affect and research from the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence (Dr. Marc Brackett). Our emotional state is mapped across two fundamental dimensions: Energy and Pleasantness:

🔴 HIGH ENERGY / UNPLEASANT (Red Quadrant)
Anger, anxiety, stress, panic, frustration, overwhelm. Signals that mobilize your nervous system to defend boundaries or respond to challenge.

🟡 HIGH ENERGY / PLEASANT (Yellow Quadrant)
Joy, excitement, inspiration, optimism, pride. States of creative flow, openness, vitality, and social connection.

🔵 LOW ENERGY / UNPLEASANT (Blue Quadrant)
Sadness, loneliness, burnout, grief, apathy, boredom. Helpful indicators that your body needs rest, healing, sleep, or comforting support.

🟢 LOW ENERGY / PLEASANT (Green Quadrant)
Calm, gratitude, contentment, tranquility, balance. The restorative zone of cognitive clarity, regeneration, and presence.

Explore 144 rich, nuanced emotions to build real emotional granularity.

◆ A 30-SECOND CHECK-IN: HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap "Check In" on your home screen.
2. Select your quadrant and pinpoint up to 3 precise emotions on the interactive mood plane.
3. Add context with a couple of taps: who you are with, where you are, and what you are doing.
4. Optionally record a private thought or reflection in your journal.
5. Done! Naming your feelings calms the amygdala and restores agency.

◆ 100% PRIVATE & ON-DEVICE ONLY
• Zero-Data Collection: No cloud servers, no account creation, no sign-ups.
• Sandboxed Storage: All your entries, notes, and patterns stay 100% local on your device.
• Zero Trackers: No third-party ads, no Meta Pixel, no Google Analytics.
• Complete Sovereignty: Clear your history or export your reflections anytime.

◆ JOURNAL & PATTERN INSIGHTS
Track trends over days, weeks, and months. Discover which people, activities, and environments nourish your vitality — and which deplete it.
```
